Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

This policy covers dsylvester.io and dsylvester.ai, the personal website of Davis Sylvester, an individual based in Van Alstyne, Texas. It explains what information the site collects and what happens to it.

What the site collects

The site has no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies. It only collects what you send through the contact form:

  • your name and, if you give it, your company;
  • the reason you are getting in touch and your message;
  • how you want a reply (email, SMS text message or WhatsApp) and the address or phone number for it;
  • the IP address the request came from, used only to limit how many messages can be sent per hour.

How it is used

Your message is forwarded to Davis so he can read it and reply. It is delivered to his own email, phone (SMS and WhatsApp), Telegram, Slack and Microsoft Teams accounts. Text messages sent by this site go only to Davis's own phone number. If you ask for a reply by SMS or WhatsApp, Davis uses your number only to answer your message, and never for marketing.

Mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

Your information is never sold or rented. It is processed only by the services that deliver and store messages: Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage, email, SMS and WhatsApp delivery), Meta (WhatsApp), Telegram, Slack and Microsoft. Information may be disclosed if the law requires it.

How long it is kept

A copy of each contact message is stored in Amazon Web Services for one year and then deleted automatically. The per-IP request counters expire within hours.

Your choices

To ask what is stored about you, or to have it deleted, email dsylvesteriii@gmail.com. If you receive a text from Davis and no longer want to, reply STOP. See the terms of use for the full SMS terms.
